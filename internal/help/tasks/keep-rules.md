title: "Keep the rules I added to a sandbox"
concepts: [policy-file, base-policy]
commands:
  - "brig policy diff dev webapp"
  - "brig policy save dev webapp"
show:
  href: "/sandboxes"
---
1. Open the sandbox, then Policy.
2. Press Save to file to preview what changes in its policy file, then save.

Saving does not change other sandboxes and does not commit the file.
